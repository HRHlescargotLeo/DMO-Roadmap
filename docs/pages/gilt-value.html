Prototype 4 of 5 · Buying gilts · Value a holding

What we're proposing

Choose a gilt, enter how much of it you hold, and see an indicative value at today's price, with the interest it pays and when it's repaid. It uses the same daily prices the Purchase and Sale Service publishes now.

Why

The prices are already published, as the D10B and D9D reports, but as file downloads that assume the reader knows what "per £100 nominal" means and can do the multiplication. Someone who has inherited a gilt certificate, which is probably a common reason to land on these pages, just wants to know what it's worth.

Prices are sample figures. Open this page from a row in gilts in issue to arrive with the gilt already chosen.

    This is an index-linked gilt, so the amount repaid and the interest rise with inflation. The value shown doesn't include that uplift.
