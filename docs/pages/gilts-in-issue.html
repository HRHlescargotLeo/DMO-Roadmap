Prototype 1 of 5 · Gilt market data

What we're proposing

Put gilts in issue on the page as a table, for whichever date the user picks, with filters for gilt type and maturity, a search by name or ISIN, a simple chart and the downloads underneath. The same template would then carry the other D-code reports, and a data catalogue lists every dataset in one place.

Why

At the moment the Gilts in Issue page is four paragraphs of text with links in them. The report behind it is a date field and three buttons, PDF, Excel and Word, so the figure most people came for never appears on a web page at all. Analysts will still want the spreadsheet, and this keeps it. But a journalist checking one gilt, or a screen reader user, shouldn't need to download a file to read a number. Word is a curious choice for a table of figures, and CSV, which the Help page says the site supports, isn't offered.

Gilt names are real; amounts, ISINs and dates of first issue are sample data. Try a weekend date, or a date with a mistake in it.
