Prototype 4 of 5 · Buying gilts · Eligibility check

What we're proposing

Three to five questions, one per page, that tell someone whether they can use the Purchase and Sale Service and what to do next. People who can't are pointed to a broker or their bank, rather than finding out from a returned form.

Why

The rules are all on the site, but scattered through the Q&A: live in the UK, be in the Approved Group to buy (not to sell), don't hold in CREST, pay electronically above £1,000, get the form in by 10am. Someone has to read the whole page to assemble them. The Q&A also notes that an unclear or incomplete form is rejected and sent back, which a check like this should make less likely.

Try answering "No" to the first question, or say you want to sell.
