Prototype 2 of 5 · Gilt operations · Email alerts

What we're proposing

A short sign-up for email alerts by topic, with a choice of every update or a daily summary, plus an RSS feed for each topic. It follows the GOV.UK pattern of one thing per page, then a check-your-answers page before anything is sent.

Why

We found no email or RSS sign-up on the homepage or the Publications page. Anyone who needs to know the moment a result is out has to keep checking the site. Other sovereign debt offices offer alerts as a matter of course.

Nothing is sent from this prototype.
