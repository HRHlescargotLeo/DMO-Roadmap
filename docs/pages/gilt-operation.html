Prototype 2 of 5 · Gilt operations

What we're proposing

A page for each auction, syndication and tender that carries it from the calendar to the result. The key facts sit at the top, a timeline shows where it has got to, the result is published in the page itself, and the PDFs are attached underneath for the record. Every notice links back here, and people can sign up to be told when the result is out.

Why

Each operation currently produces a run of separate PDFs. The 4⅞% Treasury Gilt 2036 auction on 29 September has five rows in the Publications table, the announcement and prospectus a week earlier, then the result, the post-auction option result and a re-opening prospectus on the day. Nothing ties them together, and the result, which is the thing the market is waiting for, is a 67KB PDF. GDS guidance is to publish in HTML first; this is also simply quicker to read.

Gilt names, dates and sizes for September come from DMO publications. Results and ISINs are sample figures. Open other operations from the calendar.
