Prototype 2 of 5 · Gilt operations · Calendar

What we're proposing

The issuance calendar as a table on the page, filterable by method and gilt type, with past operations and their results in the same place. Dealers can subscribe to it in Outlook or Google Calendar, and download it as CSV. A banner flags anything live, such as the current consultation.

Why

The Issuance Calendar page says the calendar is published each March and updated quarterly, then links to a report. The consultation on the 7 October tender is currently a line in the homepage news tab. For something with a closing time, that's easy to miss.

Operations up to 7 October are taken from DMO publications. Dates after that are sample and marked as such.
