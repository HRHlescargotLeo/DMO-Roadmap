Prototype 5 of 5 · PWLB rates

What we're proposing

Today's PWLB rates in a table on the rates page, by loan type and term, with the time they were published and the change since the last set. Underneath, an estimator gives indicative repayments at those rates, and a separate page sets out how to apply for a loan.

Why

The Current Interest Rates page explains that fixed rates are published at 9:30am and 12:30pm, then sends readers elsewhere for the rates themselves. It also asks people to "please click here" for the rentcharges guidance and gives two phone numbers in different formats in one sentence. Local authority treasurers check these rates twice a day and know exactly what they want, so the page ought to give it to them first.

All rates on this page are sample figures. The margins used for standard and HRA rates are assumptions for the prototype.
