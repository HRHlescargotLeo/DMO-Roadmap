Prototype 5 of 5 · PWLB rates · Apply for a loan

What we're proposing

One "Apply for a PWLB loan" page, set out step by step, with a single stable link to whichever application form is current. Who can borrow, what to check first, the deadlines and what happens after you apply all sit on the same page.

Why

Application forms are published as PDFs whose names change each month, "pwlb loan application form august 2026 major", then a template for September. Anyone who bookmarked last month's form is now using the wrong one. The steps themselves are spread across the Lending Arrangements, Concessionary Rates and What's New pages.

Deadlines and steps here are placeholders in square brackets. They need to come from the DMO's lending arrangements.
