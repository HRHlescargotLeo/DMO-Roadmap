Question for the DMOWe've dated the original 2026-27 remit "March 2026" from memory of the usual timetable. Which date should it carry, and should each in-year revision show what changed?

How the remit is set

Before each financial year, HM Treasury sets out how much the government needs to borrow and how the DMO should raise it. The remit can be revised during the year, usually after a Budget or fiscal event.

What do short, medium and long mean?
Short conventional gilts are due to be repaid within 7 years, medium within 7 to 15 years and long in more than 15 years. Index-linked gilts pay interest and repay capital in line with inflation.
What is the unallocated amount?
Part of the year's sales the DMO can assign to any type or method later in the year, depending on demand.
