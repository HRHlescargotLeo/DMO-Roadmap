Quick win 7 of 23 · Sensible defaults on Publications

What we're proposing

Make the Publications page open on the last 12 months, newest first, add a keyword box, and limit the year menus to years that have publications. The filters stay in the address, so a filtered list can be bookmarked.

Why

The Publications link in the main menu has its start date fixed at April 2025, so it opens with 685 results across 35 pages, and the date will only get staler. The start and end year menus each list 127 years, back to 1900.
