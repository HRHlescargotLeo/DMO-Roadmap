Quick win 11 of 23 · One date style

What we're proposing

Write every date the same way, "30 September 2026", as the GOV.UK style guide sets out, in lists, tables, news and documents.

Why

The Publications table writes dates as "30-September-2026". The homepage news uses "(30/09/2026)", and the report pages take "30/09/2026". Three styles on three pages is a small thing, but it is the kind of small thing people notice on a site whose job is precision.
