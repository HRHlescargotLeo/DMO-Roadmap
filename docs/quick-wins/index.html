23 quick wins for dmo.gov.uk

Small, separate changes that can each be made on their own, without the larger improvements. Each one shows the page as it is today next to the proposed change.

Data and reports

  1. QW1

    CSV and open-format downloads

    Every data report offers CSV and OpenDocument alongside Excel, with format and size on each link.

    Try: Compare the two sets of download options.

    Open quick win 1
  2. QW2

    Show when the data is as at

    A "data as at" and "next update" line on every data page and report.

    Try: Look for when the figures were last updated in each version.

    Open quick win 2
  3. QW3

    A GOV.UK date input on report pages

    Day, month and year boxes with a hint and clear error messages, in place of the single date field and pop-up calendar.

    Try: Enter 31 2 2026 in the proposed version, then press the button.

    Open quick win 3

How to review

  • Each quick win stands alone: "Today" is rebuilt from what we saw on dmo.gov.uk, and "Proposed" shows only the change.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between quick wins, or switch to the big improvements. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each quick win proposes and why.
  • Some of these also appear inside the big improvements. They are shown here because they can be done on their own, on the current site.
