Quick win 4 of 23 · The remit figures on the Remit page

What we're proposing

Put the current remit at the top of the Financing Remit page: the total, the split by type and maturity, and the date of the latest revision, with the explanation of how the remit is set underneath.

Why

The page explains how HM Treasury sets the remit, then links to a PDF of progress, a page of announcements and an Excel file. It never says what the remit is. A short table, updated when the remit is revised, answers the question most people arrive with.

Today

Before the end of each financial year HM Treasury sets an annual financing remit for the Debt Management Office for the following financial year. The remit specifies the annual total of gilt sales planned for the financial year and the breakdown between index-linked and conventional gilts.

  • Progress against the current remit
  • Full details and announcements relating to the current and previous remits
  • Composition of gilt issuance in past remits
