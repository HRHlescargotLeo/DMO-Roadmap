Quick win 8 of 23 · Tidy the publication type list

What we're proposing

Group the publication types under the area they belong to, and list each one once within its group, so the menu reads as a set of choices rather than a database export.

Why

The Publication type menu has 31 options in one long list. "Annual Reports and Accounts" appears three times, and "Consultation Papers", "Formulae and Examples" and "Operational Rules" twice each, with nothing to say which is which.
