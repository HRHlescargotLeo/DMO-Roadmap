Five ideas for dmo.gov.uk

Clickable prototypes of five improvements to the UK Debt Management Office website, one for each of its main audiences. They follow GOV.UK Design System patterns and aim at WCAG 2.2 AA, dressed in the DMO's own look: Lato, charcoal, the red rule and square corners.

The prototypes

  1. Gilt market data
    1

    Gilt market data on the page

    Gilts in issue as a table you can filter, sort and search, for any date, with an as-at stamp, a chart and open-format downloads. A data catalogue lists every dataset and the API.

    Try: show index-linked gilts only, then change the date to 3 May 2025.

    Open prototype
  2. Gilt operations
    2

    A gilt operation, from notice to result

    One page per auction, syndication or tender with the result in HTML and the documents attached, an operations calendar people can subscribe to, and email alerts.

    Try: open the calendar, include September's results, then sign up for alerts.

    Open prototype
  3. Remit at a glance
    3

    The remit at a glance

    The 2026-27 remit and progress against it, on the Remit page and on a reworked homepage with headline figures, the next operations and links grouped by audience.

    Try: open the homepage and follow the next operation.

    Open prototype
  4. Buying gilts
    4

    Buying gilts as an individual

    A step-by-step guide in place of the retail Q&A, an eligibility check for the Purchase and Sale Service, and a tool to value a holding.

    Try: run the eligibility check as someone who wants to buy but isn't in the Approved Group.

    Open prototype
  5. PWLB rates
    5

    PWLB rates and borrowing

    Today's PWLB rates on the page with the time they were set, a repayment estimator, and a step-by-step "Apply for a loan" page with one stable link to the form.

    Try: switch to EIP loans, then estimate a £5 million, 25-year EIP loan.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • Everything interactive works: filters, sorting, the date input and its error messages, the calendar subscription, the alerts sign-up, the eligibility questions and both calculators. Links to pages outside these prototypes go nowhere by design.
  • The model has two tabs: these five big improvements, and 23 quick wins that can each be done on their own. Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes or switch tabs. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for the DMO (red). Notes start switched off.
  • Gilt names, operation dates and the 2026-27 remit are real. Amounts in issue, ISINs, prices, auction results, sales to date and PWLB rates are sample figures, marked "sample" wherever they appear.
  • Every page is built on GOV.UK patterns: underlined links, a strong focus state, error summaries, date inputs, tables with proper headers, and one question per page where we ask for input. Try them with a keyboard.
  • Works on a phone: open this page on your mobile to check the layouts.
