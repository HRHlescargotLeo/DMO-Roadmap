What we're proposing

A homepage that answers the questions people arrive with: what's the remit, how far through it are we, when is the next operation, and is anything live. Below that, links are grouped by who's asking, investors, local authorities and members of the public, rather than by how the DMO is organised.

Why

Today the homepage opens with "The DMO is an Executive Agency of HM Treasury", in a heading smaller than the paragraph beneath it, followed by news tabs and six cards describing the DMO's responsibilities. It doesn't give a single figure or say when the next auction is. Its page title is "Home", which is what shows in every browser tab and bookmark.

The remit figure is real; sales to date and later operation dates are sample.
