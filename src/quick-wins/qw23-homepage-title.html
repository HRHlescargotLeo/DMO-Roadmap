Quick win 23 of 23 · Name the homepage

What we're proposing

Change the homepage's page title from "Home" to "UK Debt Management Office", and set a title pattern for every page type ("Gilts in issue – UK Debt Management Office").

Why

The homepage's title is "Home". That is what appears in the browser tab, in bookmarks and in search engine results, and it is the first thing a screen reader announces when the page loads. It is about as small a change as a website can have.
