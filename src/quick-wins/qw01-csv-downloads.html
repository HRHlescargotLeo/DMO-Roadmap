Quick win 1 of 23 · CSV and open-format downloads

What we're proposing

Add CSV and OpenDocument (ODS) to every data report, label each download with its format and size, and drop Word for tables of figures. The report pages themselves stay as they are.

Why

The gilts in issue report offers PDF, Excel and Word. Word is an odd place to put a table of numbers, and CSV, which the Help page lists among the formats the site supports, isn't offered. Government's open standards expect data to be published in CSV or ODF, so this is as much about meeting the standard as about convenience.
