Quick win 21 of 23 · An H1 that names the page

What we're proposing

Make every page's H1 name that page, show the section as a caption above it, and give each page a matching title in the browser tab.

Why

On the Gilts in Issue page the main heading is "Gilt Market", the name of the section, so every page in the section shares one H1. Screen reader users often navigate by headings, and search engines lean on them too.
