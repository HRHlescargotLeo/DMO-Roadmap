Quick win 15 of 23 · Descriptive link text

What we're proposing

Rewrite links so the words say where they go, starting with the PWLB pages, and run a crawl for "click here", "here" and bare URLs across the site.

Why

The PWLB Current Interest Rates page says "For more information on Rentcharges, please click here - www.gov.uk/guidance/rentcharges" and "Acquisition of Land Compensation Rate can be seen here." Screen reader users often move between links out of context, and a list of links reading "here" tells them nothing. It is also one of the WCAG checks that automated tools catch most easily.
