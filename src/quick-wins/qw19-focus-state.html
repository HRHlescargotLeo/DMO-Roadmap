Quick win 19 of 23 · A visible focus state

What we're proposing

Apply the GOV.UK focus state (a yellow highlight with a dark underline) to every link, button and form field, and test it at 200% and 400% zoom.

Why

The accessibility statement itself notes that keyboard focus on the search button disappears at 200% zoom. Keyboard users need to see where they are on the page at all times, and the yellow focus state is one of the most tested parts of the GOV.UK Design System.
