Quick win 2 of 23 · Show when the data is as at

What we're proposing

Add a short stamp to every data page and report: the close of business the figures relate to, and when they will next be updated.

Why

The gilts in issue report notes that it runs for UK working days, but none of the data pages we looked at says when its figures were last updated or when they will change next. For people pricing or trading on these numbers, the timing is half of the information.
