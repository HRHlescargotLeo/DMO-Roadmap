Quick win 17 of 23 · An up-to-date accessibility statement

What we're proposing

Audit the main templates against WCAG 2.2 AA, then rewrite the statement with what fails, a plan with dates for fixing it, how the site was tested, and a yearly review.

Why

The statement was prepared on 8 November 2021, last reviewed on 21 April 2023, and measures the site against WCAG 2.1. The Government Digital Service has monitored public sector sites against WCAG 2.2 since October 2024, and the regulations expect statements to be kept up to date. Testing is a weekly automated scan of 125 pages, which catches a good deal but not everything.
