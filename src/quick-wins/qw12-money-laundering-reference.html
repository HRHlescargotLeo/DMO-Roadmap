What we're proposing

Correct the reference in the Approved Group answer, and check every other legal reference on the retail pages while we are there. The wording should be agreed with the DMO's compliance team.

Why

The answer to "What does the Approved Group represent?" cites the Money Laundering Regulations 2007. Those were replaced in 2017 by the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations. It is a one-line fix, and on a government site it is worth making.
