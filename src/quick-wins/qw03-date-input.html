Quick win 3 of 23 · A GOV.UK date input on report pages

What we're proposing

Replace the single dd/mm/yyyy field and its pop-up calendar on the report pages with the GOV.UK date input: three labelled boxes, a hint with an example, and error messages that say what to fix.

Why

Today's field is labelled "Gilts in issue at COB:*", which assumes the reader knows COB means close of business, and the calendar pop-up's Cancel and OK buttons are set in 11.9px text. The GOV.UK pattern has been tested with a wide range of users, including people using screen readers and voice control, and it fails politely when someone gets a date wrong.
