Proposed

The price you pay is quoted per £100 nominal and excludes accrued interest. Gilts bought in the ex-dividend period do not receive the next interest payment.

What does "nominal" mean?
The amount the government repays when the gilt redeems. It's printed on your certificate and is usually different from what you paid.
What is the ex-dividend period?
A short period before each interest payment. If you buy during it, the interest goes to the previous owner.
