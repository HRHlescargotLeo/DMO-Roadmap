What we're proposing

Add a "Next gilt operations" strip to the homepage, under the introduction, showing the next three or four operations from the calendar, each linking to its announcement.

Why

The homepage has Latest News and Most Popular tabs and a card for the Gilt Operations Calendar, but nowhere on it does it say when the next auction is. For the people who use the site most, that is probably the first question. The strip reads from the same calendar data, so nobody has to maintain it by hand.
