Quick win 22 of 23 · A clear type scale

What we're proposing

Adopt a stepped type scale (36, 27, 22, 19, 16 and 14 pixels) so page titles lead and section headings sit clearly below them, in proportions close to the GOV.UK scale.

Why

The homepage heading, "The DMO is an Executive Agency of HM Treasury", is set at 22px, and the paragraph beneath it at 24px, so the heading is the smaller of the two. H1s and H2s across the site are both 22px.
