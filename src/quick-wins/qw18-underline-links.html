Quick win 18 of 23 · Underline links

What we're proposing

Underline every link in content and tables and colour them in the DMO red, with a thicker underline on hover.

Why

Links in the Publications table are the same near-black as the text around them, with no underline, so nothing but their position says they are links. WCAG asks that colour isn't the only way to tell a link apart, and here there isn't even colour.
